Objective and topology

The lab separates forwarding in the switch data plane from decisions made manually or by an external controller. The switch starts with no controller and a user-space datapath.

Experiments

Step 0 — Empty switch observed

The flow table was empty and h1 to h2 had 100% packet loss.

Step 1 — Hub observed

pingall had 0% loss, the flood counter increased from 5 to 27 packets, and h3 captured all six ICMP packets in the h1-to-h2 exchange.

Step 2 — Manual forwarding incomplete

Replace flooding with explicit ARP and destination-MAC forwarding rules.

Step 3 — Firewall and priority incomplete

Compare an overlapping default-priority firewall rule with a priority-40000 drop.

Step 4 — Connect the controller incomplete

Connect ovs-testcontroller and inspect its table-miss and reactive forwarding entries.

Step 5 — Controller failure incomplete

Stop the controller, compare existing and new conversations, and wait for flow expiry.

Step 6 — Secure versus standalone incomplete

Change to standalone mode and test the local learning-switch fallback.
